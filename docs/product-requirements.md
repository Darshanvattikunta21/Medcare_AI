# Product Requirements

## Source

This repository structure is derived from `MediCare_AI_Documentation.docx` dated May 2025.

## Core personas

- General public users ordering OTC and prescription medicines
- Chronic condition patients needing refill continuity and medication history
- Elderly users needing accessible flows, OCR assistance, and simplified UX

## Primary modules

1. AI symptom and disease engine
2. AI prescription OCR and verification
3. Drug interaction detection
4. Smart pharmacy selection
5. Real-time delivery tracking
6. Patient health profile
